A simulation shows what follows from its assumptions. It is not a forecast, and nothing here places a trade.
How the simulation works
  • Option value: the Black-Scholes model, with the contract's implied volatility taken from its market price, so it starts at the market.
  • Each scenario is a scripted price story (for example: reach resistance, break it, follow through) with random noise around it. PLAY shows one roll; the shaded band holds 8 of 10 rolls.
  • Volume doesn't choose a direction. It scales how far the move goes (by the square root of volume), and the surge fades by half every 2 days.
  • Volatility rises 3% for each 1% the price falls, eases 1.5% for each 1% it rises, and gets a small lift from a volume surge.
  • Resistance and support start as the nearest 20-day or 60-day high and low. Type your own levels to change them.
  • Prices are CBOE delayed quotes. You pay the ask; "worth" is the midpoint, so a new pick starts slightly negative.
