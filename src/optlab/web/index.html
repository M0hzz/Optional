How the simulation works
  • Option value: the Black-Scholes model, with the contract's implied volatility taken from its market price, so it starts at the market.
  • Each scenario is a scripted price story (for example: reach resistance, break it, follow through) with random noise around it. PLAY shows one roll; the shaded band holds 8 of 10 rolls.
  • Volume doesn't choose a direction. It scales how far the move goes (by the square root of volume), and the surge fades by half every 2 days.
  • Volatility rises 3% for each 1% the price falls, eases 1.5% for each 1% it rises, and gets a small lift from a volume surge.
  • Indicators use the stock's last year of daily closes, then the simulated days, so they change as a scenario plays: EMA 9/21 (short trend), SMA 50 and 200 (medium and long trend), MACD 12/26/9 (momentum), RSI 14 (overbought above 70, oversold below 30), Bollinger 20/2 (range and squeezes). TREND counts the four trend signals: 3 or more pointing the same way is an uptrend or downtrend, otherwise mixed.
  • Home shows every position's live and simulated P&L together, the symbols you watch, and chains of linked symbols. The monitor uses Yahoo quotes; option prices on tabs use CBOE's 15-minute delayed feed, so a symbol's price can differ slightly between the two.
  • Chains compare an anchor with each linked symbol over the days both traded: correlation and beta of daily returns over 60 days, the difference in 5- and 20-day returns, and today's gap (the anchor's change minus beta × the link's change). Flags: trends disagree, a gap of 1.5% or more, or correlation under 0.3.
  • Timeframes: 5m, 15m and 1h bars cover the last 10 days, month and 6 months of regular trading hours; weekly bars are built from 5 years of daily ones. Indicators, the trend read and the levels use whichever you pick. On an intraday timeframe a scenario plays out over hours, one bar per step, with a trading session counted as one day of time decay.
  • Tabs are separate simulations, saved in this browser. COPY TAB starts a new one from the current contract.
  • Resistance and support come from swing highs and lows over the RANGE you pick (1M to 5Y): swing points within half a day's average range of each other count as one level, and each is a touch. The nearest above and below drive the scenarios; tap another, or type your own.
  • Prices are CBOE delayed quotes. You pay the ask; "worth" is the midpoint, so a new pick starts slightly negative.
